About

I hold a Master of Science in Forensic Science from Bundelkhand University, with a focus on digital forensics and the physical sciences, following a Bachelor of Science in Forensic Science from Aditya Degree and PG College. Since qualifying UGC‑NET Forensic Science for PhD admission in 2025, I have worked across government and private forensic laboratories, a cybersecurity internship, and academic research — and now teach as an Assistant Professor while continuing my own research into digital evidence integrity and forensic toxicology.
