About

I started out building small tools for friends' businesses and found I liked the whole arc of a project more than any single part of it — talking to the people who'd use something, sketching it, then staying on through the messy last 10% of shipping it.

These days I split my time between product design and front-end engineering, usually on small teams where those two things aren't separate jobs. Outside of work I restore old furniture and take too many photos of my dog.
